Another year of model release and AI agents in AI. The year saw incremental model improvements, Google gaining ground in terms of model use, coding ai agents becoming more reliable, questions on the return of investment on AI and concerns around an AI bubble. My own observation, in a limited way, touch upon all of these topics. My notes are based on my readings, explorations and conversations with others.

# Definitions
 - Agents vs LLM calls
 - Agent is an ecosystem - LLM APIs, Memory, Tools (MCP/Skills), Agent Harness, File system use, Guardrails
 - Agents vs RPA
 - Everyone is selling the "One AI Platform to rule them all"

# On Software Engineering
 - Coding agents are here to stay and upskilling is needed
 - This will impact or has started impacting team dynamics
 - Developers have to pay to get better tools. Will an average developer with $200 per month to spare do better than an expert?

# Impact of AI outside coding
